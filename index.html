04 / The feeling

Morocco is a country of contrasts.

Sea to desert. Ancient medina to contemporary city. A quiet cup of tea to a market full of noise.

Its landscapes stretch from the Atlantic and Mediterranean coasts to the High Atlas. Its food, architecture and hospitality carry layers of history without feeling frozen in it.
