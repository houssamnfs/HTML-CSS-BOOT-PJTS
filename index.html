The feeling

Morocco is more than a destination.

It is the sound of a medina waking up, the first mint tea of the afternoon, a road cutting through the Atlas and a table that somehow always has room for one more person.

The country's landscapes stretch from sea level on the Atlantic coast to the High Atlas, while its cities preserve centuries of architecture, craft, food and tradition.
